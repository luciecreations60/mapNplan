Three days in Paris: a practical first-time itinerary

A calm and flexible framework for organising a first visit to Paris with essential neighbourhood suggestions and planning tips.

Overview

Planning 3 days in Paris is easier when each day focuses on a compact part of the city. This reduces unnecessary transport, leaves time for meals and queues, and makes the visit more enjoyable.

Suggested itinerary

Day 1 — Historic centre and the Seine. Start with a gentle orientation around the Île de la Cité and the river. Continue towards the Latin Quarter or the Marais.

Day 2 — Museums, gardens and the west of central Paris. Reserve one major museum only if it is a priority, then combine it with a nearby garden and neighbourhood.

Day 3 — A local neighbourhood and final viewpoint. Choose an area that offers a different atmosphere, such as Montmartre or Canal Saint-Martin.

Practical planning tips

Group activities by neighbourhood and check opening times. Allow extra time for transport and queues. Use a simple daily plan: confirmed, preferred, optional.
